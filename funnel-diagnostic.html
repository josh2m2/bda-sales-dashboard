06 — Open Question
Q1
What changed between March and May?
Setter conversion drops from ~49% in March to ~33% by June across both products. This predates Evan joining the team (May 5). Something changed in that window — most likely the triage script, qualification criteria, or offer positioning. Identifying what changed is the key to reversing the trend.
07 — Conclusions
1
Setter conversion is in sustained decline across both products
Down from 48–64% (Jan–Mar) to 16–24% by July across both F2F and 6FF. Root cause — likely a script or process change between January and May — needs to be identified and corrected.
2
6FF closer show rate recovered in July — verify it's structural
6FF closer show ran at 64–66% through March–June vs F2F's 83–88%. Recovered to 85% in July. Root cause unknown — confirm what changed, that the fix is embedded, and monitor August to verify it holds.
